# Rice and Shine!
Contains the files for the game "Rice and Shine!", created for the Hack Club event **"Thirdspace"**. Play it here: https://chromalescence-labs.itch.io/rice-and-shine.

## Summary
### I. Lore
You play as Mary Rose Suarez, who manages a carinderia named “Rice and Shine” alongside her lola. Things have been this way ever since your mother went abroad to work as a caretaker in Hongkong since the passing of your late father left a heavy burden on your family. With no other choice, she left you in the care of your grandmother for as long as you can remember.

## How to Run
1. Go to this link: https://chromalescence-labs.itch.io/rice-and-shine, no need to install anything on your device.
2. Click on the "Run Game" button found on the page.
3. (Hopefully) Enjoy playing the game!
4. Feel free to give us constructive criticism - we're beginners and are always open to respectful advice.
